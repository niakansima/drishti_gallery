About

Drishti means sight, but it also means vision: the way a person chooses to see. Every painting is a conversation someone started long ago, still waiting for someone to answer. Here, works from before Independence sit beside modern masters and contemporary voices, so you can feel the whole river of art at once.

Drishti Gallery was founded by Dhrubojyoti Chatterjee, a graphic design graduate who once taught at a university. At just 20 he began running an art magazine called Monkey Art, and he kept it going for five years. Now, after 20 years in the industry and with a lasting love for animation, he is looking for fresh artists, with digital painting and comics at the top of his list. Come as you are. Leave carrying a story of your own.
